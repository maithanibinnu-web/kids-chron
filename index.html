SpaceX's Starship: the biggest rocket ever built

Starship is taller than a 30-storey building and weighs about as much as 1,000 elephants. Both of its parts can fly, land and be used again — the same way you'd reuse a favourite toy.

Its first big test flight lifted off on 20 April 2023. It didn't go fully to plan, but every launch teaches the engineers something new on the road to the Moon and Mars.

50 years of Project Tiger

India's tiger rescue turned 50. From near-disappearance in 1973, more than 70% of the world's wild tigers now live here — around 3,000 big cats across 53 reserves.

🎉 Culture · Northeast India

Siang Unying, celebrated by the Adi community

High in Arunachal Pradesh, the Adi people welcome spring with Siang Unying — a festival of food, music and togetherness. "Adi" means "hill people", and though the community has many groups, they all share one name and one celebration.

It's a reminder that some of the world's most beautiful stories are right here at home.
